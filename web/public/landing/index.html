At the counter

Will shopkeepers agree to be recorded?

Consent comes before the first recording at a shop: the shopkeeper agrees by a WhatsApp link or a paper form, and their answer is saved. If they say no, nothing is recorded, and the shop is marked “do not record” so the rep isn't asked to record there again.

The WhatsApp page reads in Hindi, English or Marathi, and we help you word the request so it sounds like your reps, not a legal notice.

Will reps feel like they're being watched?

CounterTalk listens for what the shopkeeper says: complaints, competitor offers and requests. It isn't built to score reps. Nothing reaches a team until the rep has read the visit report and confirmed it, so the rep stays in control of what's sent.

Reps get something back straight away: no visit report to type up, proof that the problems they raise reached someone, and what was fixed before they go back to the shop.

You decide who can listen to recordings, and we help you explain that to the field team.

What it does

How do you know a quote is what the shopkeeper actually said?

The AI has to say which line of the transcript each quote came from. CounterTalk then looks for those words in the transcript itself, in Devanagari or Latin script, with “चार हज़ार पाँच सौ” and “4500” counting as the same number.

A quote that's found is replaced with the transcript's own text, so what you read is what was said. A finding whose quote can't be found is never sent to a team.

What's a pre-visit brief?

A one-page look at a shop before the rep walks in: what it usually raises and orders, the last visit's problems in the shopkeeper's own words, what's still open, and what's been fixed since.

It's ready once a shop has two confirmed visits. The AI writes a short overview and what's worth raising this time, and any sentence with a number that isn't in the shop's visits is dropped.

How does Ask work?

Type a question the way you'd ask a colleague, in Hindi, English or Marathi: “Is mahine Kanpur mein small packs ki complaints?” You get the matching findings, newest first, each with the shopkeeper's quote.

The AI only turns your question into a search by category, product, competitor, area and date. CounterTalk checks that search and runs it, so the AI never reads your visits, and each team sees only its own team's findings.

How does an issue reach the right team?

Your routing rules send each kind of finding to a team: credit notes to finance, stock-outs to distribution. The rep sees those teams while reviewing the visit and can change them before confirming.

Where your rules have no team, the AI suggests one with its reason, for a person to accept. Anything still unplaced waits in a triage queue. In each team's inbox, the same problem from different shops becomes one issue, counted in shops, with an owner, a status and a due date.

What does “Fixed since your last visit” mean for the rep?

When a team resolves an issue, it writes a short note for the shop, like “your ₹4,500 credit note has been issued.” Whichever rep visits that shop next sees the fix and the note to pass on, in the shop's brief and on their Updates page.

The shopkeeper hears their complaint was acted on, and the rep sees how many of the problems they raised this month have been fixed.

Languages, network and phones

Which languages does it support?

Hindi, English, the Hinglish in between, and Marathi, including switches in the middle of a sentence. If your beats use other languages, tell us and we'll test them on your own recordings before you start.

Quotes stay in the original script, with a one-line English summary alongside.

Does it work with a poor network?

Yes. Recording doesn't need a signal. The audio waits on the phone and uploads on its own once the rep is back in coverage, so a visit to a basement shop or a one-bar village isn't lost.

Do reps need new phones?

No. It runs in the browser on the Android phones your reps already carry, so there's no app to install. All it needs is a working microphone.

Data and trust

What if the transcript gets something wrong?

The rep reads every visit report before it's sent, and can edit or delete any finding. Every insight also links to its moment in the recording, so anyone can listen and check for themselves.

Who can see our data, and is it used to train AI?

Recordings, transcripts and insights are visible only to the people you give access to. We don't use your recordings or transcripts to train AI models.

Audio is deleted automatically after the period you set. The insights and quotes stay for as long as you need them.
